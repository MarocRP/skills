Add **`how`** (model-invoked) to the **Productivity** bucket. `/how <question>` explains how a resource or subsystem works: overview, key concepts, the flow step by step across client, server and NUI, where things live, and gotchas. Narrow questions get one read-only subagent; bigger ones get two to four explorers on the `explore` model and an explainer on the `hardest tasks` model from `~/.config/ox/models.md`. Resources you don't own are read from the copy your server runs. Adapted from pstack-t3's `how` (MIT), credited in the skill's `CREDITS.md`.
