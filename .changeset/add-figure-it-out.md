Add **`figure-it-out`** (model-invoked) to the **Productivity** bucket. For a large task no other workflow covers, it states a checkable definition of done, designs a phase list with rigor scaled to the risk, runs it one verified unit at a time, and keeps a decision trail. Adapted from pstack-t3's `figure-it-out` (MIT), credited in the skill's `CREDITS.md`.
