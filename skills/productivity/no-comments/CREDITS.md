Adapted from the `no-comments` skill in [pstack-t3](https://github.com/mariusflorescu/pstack-t3/tree/main/skills/no-comments), itself adapted from [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) by Lauren Tan.
